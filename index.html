Questions

What owners ask us

We already have a QR menu. How is this different?

We're not just digitising your menu. TablePay is a branded digital experience for your business: guests discover your menu, see your brand and use your services, and as we expand, the same experience handles ordering, payments and loyalty. Think of the menu as the starting point, not the whole product. We'd be happy to show you what your own experience could look like.

Why pay monthly?

The monthly fee isn't for displaying a menu. It keeps your digital guest experience running: hosting, unlimited updates, support and the platform your guests use. As TablePay adds new services, they switch on in the same experience, without starting over with a different system.

Do guests need to download an app?

No. Everything opens in the phone's normal browser from the tap point or the QR code.

Does it replace our POS?

No. TablePay works alongside your POS. Your team keeps ringing up orders as they do now.

Can we change prices ourselves?

Yes. Update prices, add dishes or mark items sold out from your phone, or send us a WhatsApp and we'll do it for you.

Does it work for hotels?

Yes. Hotels use TablePay across rooms, restaurants, bars and spas under one brand. See TablePay for hotels.

How fast can we start?

Send a photo of your menu and your logo, and your tables can be live within 24 hours.
