Questions

What owners ask us

Do guests need to download an app?

No. Everything opens in the phone's normal browser from the tap chip or the QR code.

Does it replace our POS?

No. TablePay works alongside your POS. Your team keeps ringing up orders as they do now.

Can we change prices ourselves?

Yes. Update prices, add dishes or mark items sold out from your phone, or send us a WhatsApp and we'll do it for you.

What if a guest's phone has no NFC?

Every talker also has a QR code, which any smartphone camera can scan.

How much does it cost?

A one-time setup fee and a simple monthly fee, based on your number of tables. Every restaurant gets a 14-day trial once its tables are set up, and a clear quote before anything is paid.

How fast can we start?

Send a photo of your menu and your tables can be live within 24 hours.
